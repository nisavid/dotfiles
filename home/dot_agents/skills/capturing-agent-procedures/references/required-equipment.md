# Resolve missing required equipment

Use this branch when a current, applicable task instruction requires agent equipment that the active harness cannot resolve. A current requirement for intentionally retired or unsupported equipment still needs reconciliation. Historical explanations, intentional omissions without an applicable current invocation, and future prerequisites not currently invoked remain outside this branch.

## Bootstrap unavailable helpers

If capture itself or a required ownership, review, or evaluation helper is unavailable, use available maintained task instructions and current authority for the bounded lookup, tracking, and dispatch below. Record the missing helper and its unmet evidence requirement. Use only available controls; keep the unavailable helper out of the invocation chain and its review or qualification unestablished.

## 1. Check the required route

Check the active equipment catalog, relevant namespaces and supported aliases, documented discovery roots, and relevant symlinks. Keep discovery scoped to the requirement, distinguishing content present on disk from equipment exposed to this harness. Record the instruction source, checked routes, and observation limits.

Record the discovery result:

- A supported resolution of the current requirement.
- A concrete current mismatch.
- An inconclusive result naming the smallest observation or owner action needed to settle it. Keep that result open until the evidence arrives.

Initial discovery is complete when it resolves the current requirement through a supported route. A concrete mismatch continues through owned repair; an inconclusive result stays open for its required observation or owner action.

Partial or failed observation establishes neither absence nor usability. An initial supported alias resolution needs no repair. An alias found during an established repair does not replace its owner's acceptance or consumer checks.

## 2. Reconcile sources and ownership

Use `extending-managed-skills` for source and manager classification and `resolving-workflow-ownership` for decision and actuation ownership. Bootstrap as above when either helper is unavailable.

Find the maintained instruction and equipment sources, settled disposition or replacement decisions, current tickets, and active writers. Cover relevant result pages; one empty or truncated list is insufficient. Reuse existing repair work and coordinate its smallest safe seam with the active writer. Record an acknowledged owner or the concrete ownership question that remains open.

## 3. Track and dispatch work when action is due

If the accepted repair's owner, scope, and remaining work are unchanged, retain that state without another comment, update, or dispatch. Change or redispatch only when new evidence, changed scope, lost ownership, or a required next action warrants it.

When action is due, read the ticket-owning repository's policy before writes. Within current authority, update existing work or create the smallest governed repair, then dispatch the authorized action through the supported coordination route. Confirm that the receiving owner or worker accepted the scope and closeout criteria; a ticket link alone does not establish execution.

When a decision, writer, or actuator is unavailable, retain the concrete draft or next action and name what is needed to proceed. Continue independent authorized work.

## 4. Keep immediate work and canonical repair separate

Use an immediate supported route only when the governing workflow or operator authorizes it. Record its limits and keep the canonical mismatch open.

- For settled retirement, remove current invocations through their maintained owner.
- For uncertain replacement coverage, obtain the owner's decision.

Preserve dated evidence and managed source. Installation, restoration, and host changes retain their separate authority; the mismatch grants none of them.

## 5. Verify the repair at its consumer

Follow the owning source's review, checks, publication, and targeted materialization contract. Have the affected consumer locate the reviewed source revision and invoke the corrected route, or verify a settled intentional omission against its current instruction. Record source, publication, installation, and consumer results separately, and return changed evidence or useful corrections to the producer.

An established repair is complete only under its owner's acceptance contract, including verified canonical correction and affected consumption. A fallback, draft, source pass, merge, or newly discovered alias cannot supply that acceptance. Keep delegated repair work with its accepted owner and remaining action until the required evidence arrives. Capture reusable procedure corrections through the ordinary capture and consumer contract.
