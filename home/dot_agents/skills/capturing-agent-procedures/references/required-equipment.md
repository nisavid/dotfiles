# Resolve A Missing Required Route

Use this branch when a current, applicable task instruction requires agent equipment that the active harness cannot resolve. Historical explanations, intentional omissions without an applicable current invocation, and future prerequisites not currently invoked remain outside this branch. A current requirement to use intentionally retired or unsupported equipment still needs reconciliation.

If this skill or a required ownership, review, or evaluation helper is itself unavailable, use available maintained task instructions and current authority to perform the bounded source/owner lookup, governed tracking, and supported dispatch below. Record the missing helper and its unresolved evidence gate. Continue through available controls without recursively invoking the missing route or claiming its review or qualification.

1. **Check the required route.** Check the active equipment catalog, the referenced name's namespaces and supported aliases, and its documented discovery roots. Resolve relevant symlinks and distinguish content present on disk from equipment exposed to this harness. Keep discovery scoped to the requirement. Record the instruction source, checked route, and limits. Finish with a supported resolution, a concrete current mismatch, or an inconclusive discovery result with the smallest observation or owner action needed to settle it. Partial or failed observation proves neither absence nor usability. Initial supported alias resolution needs no repair; resolving an established repair still requires its owner's acceptance and consumer checks.
2. **Reconcile ownership.** Use `extending-managed-skills` for source and manager classification and `resolving-workflow-ownership` for decision and actuation joins. Apply the bootstrap above when either helper is unavailable. Find the maintained instruction and equipment sources, settled disposition or replacement decisions, current tickets, and active writers. Cover relevant result pages rather than treating one empty or truncated list as proof. Reuse existing repair work and coordinate its smallest safe seam; retain an acknowledged owner or a concrete unresolved owner join.
3. **Track and dispatch changed work.** Read the ticket-owning repository's policy before writes. Within current authority, update existing work or create the smallest governed repair and dispatch the authorized action through the supported coordination route. Confirm the receiving owner or worker accepted its scope and closeout criteria; a ticket link alone does not establish execution. If the existing accepted repair, owner, scope, and remaining joins are unchanged, retain that state without another comment, update, or dispatch. Change or redispatch only when new evidence, changed scope, lost ownership, or a required next action warrants it. For an unavailable decision, writer, or actuator, retain the concrete draft or next action and report the join; continue independent authorized work.
4. **Separate immediate work from repair.** Use a supported route only when the governing workflow or operator authorizes it. Record its limits and keep the canonical mismatch open. Settled retirement calls for removing current invocations through their maintained owner; uncertain replacement coverage calls for its owner's decision. Preserve dated evidence and managed source. The mismatch grants no installation, restoration, or host-mutation authority.
5. **Verify the repair at its consumer.** Follow the owning source's review, checks, publication, and targeted materialization contract. Have the affected consumer locate the reviewed source revision and invoke the corrected route, or verify the settled intentional omission against its current instruction. Record source, publication, installation, and consumer results separately. Route changed evidence or useful corrections back to the producer; a fallback, draft, source pass, merge, or newly discovered alias cannot close an established repair without its required acceptance evidence.

Initial discovery is complete when it resolves the current requirement through a supported route. An established repair is complete only under its owner's acceptance contract, including verified canonical correction and affected consumption. Keep an inconclusive discovery join or delegated repair open with its accepted owner and remaining action until evidence arrives. Use the ordinary capture and consumer contract for reusable procedure corrections produced by the repair.
