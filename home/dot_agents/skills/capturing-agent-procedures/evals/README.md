The three nearby negative cases invoke no capture or consumer work. Initial supported alias resolution and unchanged accepted repair are application no-ops, not negative discovery.

The response scenarios are no-tools simulations. These scenarios can evaluate discovery and proposed decisions. They do not observe tracker mutations, receiving acceptance, installation, or invocation.

Keep those action observations separate from canonical source review and publication, targeted materialization, and actual affected-consumer use.
